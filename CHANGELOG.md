# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- SSH display wake, with a temporary AirPlay fallback and cleanup.
- Two menu-bar items, global shortcuts, and manual Universal Control repair.
- Optional event-based UC monitoring, login item and rotating logs.
- Known limitations: auto-recovery may misinterpret logs; AirPlay cleanup and login after reboot cannot be guaranteed; the release is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
